Hi, we are the team behind GroshUP — a mobile game designed to make financial literacy practical, engaging, and accessible for young people.

We believe learning about money shouldn't be boring or overwhelming. Through interactive scenarios, budgeting challenges, and real-life financial decisions, GroshUP helps young people learn how to manage money, make smarter financial choices, and build confidence for their future.

We are working to turn financial education into an experience people enjoy, learn from, and can apply in everyday life. Our vision is to make financial literacy accessible beyond classrooms, starting in Armenia and growing internationally.

This blog is where we'll share our journey — our progress, ideas, challenges, milestones, and the things we learn along the way.

Follow our journey, stay tuned for updates, and help us shape the future of financial education!
